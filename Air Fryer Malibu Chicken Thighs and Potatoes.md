# Air Fryer Malibu Chicken Thighs & Potatoes

Serves 2. Bone-in, skin-on store pre-seasoned "Malibu" chicken thighs (4) and yellow potatoes in the air fryer, with optional green beans.

| Ingredient | Amount | kcal | P | C | F | Fiber |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Malibu-seasoned chicken thighs, bone-in skin-on (estimate, see note) | 1.8 lb raw (816 g) → ~455 g cooked meat + skin off the bone | 1,040 | 106 | 0 | 67 | 0 |
| Yellow potatoes | 520 g raw | 400 | 10.5 | 91 | 0.5 | 11 |
| Avocado oil | 4 ml | 33 | 0 | 0 | 3.7 | 0 |
| Garlic | ~3 cloves (9 g) | 13 | 0.5 | 3 | 0 | 0 |
| **Total** | | **1,486** | **117** | **94** | **71** | **11** |
| Green beans (optional) | 150 g | 47 | 2.7 | 10.5 | 0.2 | 4 |
| **Total with green beans** | | **1,533** | **120** | **104.5** | **71** | **15** |

## Per serving (½ of batch)
- Without green beans: **~743 kcal · 58.5 g protein · 47 g carbs · 35.5 g fat · ~5.5 g fiber**
- With green beans: **~767 kcal · 60 g protein · 52 g carbs · 35.5 g fat · ~7.5 g fiber**

## Logging by weight (more accurate than ½)
- **Chicken, weighed cooked off the bone, skin on:** per 100 g ≈ 229 kcal, 23.3 P / 0 C / 14.7 F (USDA roasted thigh meat + skin).
- **Chicken, skin removed:** per 100 g ≈ 179 kcal, 24.8 P / 0 C / 8.2 F.
- Potatoes: weigh the whole cooked batch once, then scale your portion against 400 kcal / 91 C total (+ the oil/garlic).

> [!note] About the store label
> The grocery label says 4 oz = 140 kcal, 20 P / 0 C / 4 F. That matches *boneless skinless* thigh meat, not bone-in skin-on. Applied to 1.8 lb bone-in, it would claim ~144 g protein and only ~29 g fat, which overstates protein and understates fat. Estimates above use USDA values: ~80% edible yield (bone ~20%), ~70% cooked yield, skin eaten, some fat rendered out in the air fryer.

> [!tip] Lighter version
> Pull the skin before eating to save ~50 kcal and ~6.5 g fat per 100 g of chicken.
