# Extra Special Cheddar Bay Biscuits (boxed)

Red Lobster biscuit mix upgraded with extra cheese and milk instead of water. Makes about 10 biscuits. First made Nov 27, 2026 for the postponed Thanksgiving steak dinner.

## Method
1. Preheat the oven per the box. Mix the biscuit mix, shredded cheese, and milk until just combined (don't overmix).
2. Drop about 10 even scoops onto a lined baking sheet and bake per the box until golden.
3. Stir the seasoning packet into the melted butter and brush it over the hot biscuits.

## Logging tip
Biscuits that come out uneven can be weighed: weigh the whole baked batch, then log her share as grams ÷ batch grams × totals.

> [!note] Allergens
> Contains wheat, milk, and soy; the mix may contain egg and tree nuts (check for almond cross-contact).
