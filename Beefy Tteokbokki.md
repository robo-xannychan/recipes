# Beefy Tteokbokki (TJ's Tteok Bok Ki + beef and veg)

Makes 2 servings (one for Patricia, one for her husband). First made Nov 24, 2026 as a push-day dinner.

| Ingredient | Amount | kcal | P | C | F | Fiber |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Food/Pantry Labels#Trader Joe's Tteok Bok Ki (frozen)\|Trader Joe's Tteok Bok Ki]] (rice cakes + sauce packet) | 1 bag (16 oz / 454 g) | 908 | 16 | 198 | 5 | 9.7 |
| 93% lean ground beef | 1 lb raw (454 g) | 680 | 93 | 0 | 32 | 0 |
| Green cabbage, chopped | ~200 g | 50 | 2.6 | 11.6 | 0.2 | 5.0 |
| Yellow onion, sliced | ~100 g (1 small) | 40 | 1.1 | 9.3 | 0.1 | 1.7 |
| **Total** | | **1,678** | **113** | **219** | **37** | **16.4** |

## Per serving (½ of batch)
**~840 kcal · 56 g protein · 109 g carbs · 19 g fat · ~8 g fiber**

## Method
1. Brown the beef in a large skillet and break it up. 93% beef doesn't need extra oil.
2. Add the onion and cabbage and cook until softened, 4–5 minutes.
3. Add the rice cakes, the sauce packet, and the water the bag calls for. Simmer, stirring, until the rice cakes are chewy and the sauce thickens.

## Portioning for Patricia's macros
- Weigh the whole cooked pan (subtract the pan's weight), then weigh her bowl. Her share is her bowl's grams ÷ total grams × the totals above.
- A half serving is about 840 kcal, a bit over a typical push-day dinner budget of ~750. Taking about 45% of the pan gives ~755 kcal, 51P/98C/17F.
- Carb-heavy and low fat, so it works as a post-lift dinner. A fried egg on top adds ~70 kcal and 6 g protein.

> [!note] Allergens
> The TJ's kit contains soy and sesame and may contain wheat. No almonds.
