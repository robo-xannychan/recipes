# Miso Maple Butter

A sweet-salty compound butter for steak, sweet potato, biscuits, or veggies. First made Nov 27, 2026 for the postponed Thanksgiving steak dinner.

| Ingredient | Amount | kcal | P | C | F | Fiber | Sodium |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Salted butter, softened | 28 g (2 tbsp) | 200 | 0.2 | 0 | 22.7 | 0 | ~180 mg |
| [[Food/Pantry Labels#Marukome White Miso (tub)\|Marukome white miso]] | 36 g (~2 tbsp) | 48 | 2.4 | 4.8 | 1.2 | 2.4 | 1,848 mg |
| Maple syrup | 20 g (~2 tbsp) | 52 | 0 | 13.4 | 0 | 0 | ~2 mg |
| **Total (~84 g)** | | **300** | **2.6** | **18.2** | **23.9** | **2.4** | **~2,030 mg** |

## Per amount
- Per 10 g: ~36 kcal, 0.3P / 2.2C / 2.8F, 0.3 g fiber, ~240 mg sodium.
- Per 1 tsp (~5 g): ~18 kcal. Per 1 tbsp (~14 g): ~50 kcal, 0.4P / 3C / 4F.

## Method
1. Let the butter soften to room temperature.
2. Mash the butter, miso, and maple syrup together with a fork until smooth.
3. Serve soft, or roll it in plastic wrap and chill to slice.

## Logging tip
Weigh the bowl before and after serving and log the grams used at ~3.6 kcal per gram. It's salty, so a little goes a long way.

> [!note] Allergens
> Contains dairy and soy. No almonds.
