# Stovetop Cornbread Stuffing (boxed)

Makes about 6 servings. First made Nov 27, 2026 for the postponed Thanksgiving steak dinner.

| Ingredient | Amount | kcal | P | C | F | Fiber | Sodium |

## Per serving (1/6 of batch)
**~142 kcal · 4 g protein · 20 g carbs · 5 g fat · ~0.5 g fiber · ~590 mg sodium**

## Method
1. Bring the broth and butter to a boil.
2. Stir in the stuffing mix, cover, and take it off the heat for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork.
